03 Schedules The three jobs, one desk

Three schedules. Not three vendors.

This is the work inside the retainer. The price and the boundaries are on the next sheet, so nothing here is a vague “and more.”

A

Schedule A · Social ads

Meta and TikTok, managed from the same desk as the creative.

Campaigns for a small business, not a media department. I set them up, watch the spend, and change what isn’t working. You do not need a second invoice from the ad platform, or a card on file in a country you don’t live in. Media spend is planned, placed, and written down in the Friday note.

Included every month

  • Up to 2 live campaigns — Meta, TikTok, or one of each
  • $900 USD media spend, placed from this payment
  • Up to 4 of the month’s graphics cut and tested as ads
  • Friday note: what spent, what I changed, what’s next

B

Schedule B · Graphic design

Branding, social posts, and marketing materials that match the ads.

The graphics are not a separate workstream waiting on a brief I already have. Brand, posts, flyers, and ad frames come from one visual system, so the thing people tap and the page they land on look like the same company.

Included every month

  • 8 finished pieces — a post is one, a flyer or one-pager counts as two
  • Brand basics in month one if you don’t have them: mark, color, type, simple rules
  • Files you keep, exported for ads and for the site
  • I say what counts before I start, not after

C

Schedule C · Website

Designed and built to catch the click.

A small marketing site, made by the person who wrote the ad. Not a template dropped in by someone who has never seen the offer. Not a handoff to a developer you have never met.

Included

  • First eight weeks: a site of up to 5 pages, designed and built
  • After launch: 2 content or page updates a month
  • Mobile layout, a real contact path, basic titles and descriptions
  • Already have a site? Those weeks go to the pages the ads use — not a rebuild for its own sake
  • The site is yours. Hosting stays in your name
04 The month How the work actually moves

A Friday note, not a dashboard you have to learn.

The retainer is a rhythm. You always know what was spent, what changed, and what I’m making next. I don’t disappear into “the tools.”

  1. 01

    You send a brief

    What you sell, where your customers are, and the offer the ads should carry. One call if we need it — booked for overlap with US mornings or UK afternoons. I’m in Lagos, UTC+1. I don’t pretend otherwise.

  2. 02

    I send the scope

    One page. What’s in, what’s out, the start date. You are not committed because you filled in a form. You are committed when you accept that page.

  3. 03

    One USD invoice

    $2,400. $1,500 is the work. $900 is media. Work starts when it clears. Wise, Payoneer, or a bank transfer. The number on this page is the number on the invoice.

  4. 04

    Fridays are the report

    A short written note: spend, what I changed, what I’m making next week. Same person, every week. No account manager reading someone else’s numbers.

07 Fit Read this before you write

A fit if you want one desk, not a roster.

  • You want ads running, graphics that match, and a site that can take the click
  • You would rather pay one person in USD than coordinate three
  • You can share an offer, a few photos, and a straight answer on budget

This is not for

  • Checkout builds, subscriptions, or a custom app
  • Anyone who wants a guaranteed return before we’ve looked at the offer
  • A brand that needs same-day creative every week

Questions worth answering before the brief

Is the ad spend really in the same payment?

Yes. $900 of the $2,400 is media. It is spent on Meta and TikTok during the month. The Friday note lists where it went. If a platform receipt and the note disagree, that is my problem to explain, not yours to investigate across three people.

Do I need my own ad accounts?

Better if you do. I’ll help you open them so they stay in your name. If a campaign has to run through an account I manage, you still get the spend record, and you take the creatives with you if we stop.

What if I want to spend more than $900?

It goes on the same invoice, still with me. We agree the amount before the month starts. I don’t raise it quietly, and I don’t open a second billing relationship to do it.

What if I already have a website?

Then I don’t build a new one for the sake of it. The first weeks go to the pages your ads land on: the offer, the mobile layout, the contact path. Design and ads stay on the same scope.

Who owns the work?

You do. Design files, the site, and ad accounts in your name whenever the platform allows it. Hosting stays in your name. I don’t hold a login as leverage.

Can you guarantee leads or sales?

No. I can guarantee the scope, the Friday note, and that one person is accountable for the ads, the graphics, and the site. Anyone promising a return before seeing your offer and your market is guessing with your money.

How do we start, and how do we stop?

You send a brief. I reply within one business day with a yes, a no, or a question. If it’s a fit, you get a one-page scope. Nothing starts until you accept it and the invoice clears. After two months, either of us can end it with 14 days’ notice.
